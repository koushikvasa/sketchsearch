    How the agent worked

    1. Start a sweep to see its plan, searches and checks here.

    Case

      1 · Describe the moment

      Type it the way you'd tell a colleague: “a worker walking toward the forklift.” You can also draw it, or upload a sketch you made on paper.

      2 · We turn it into a stencil

      SketchSearch draws a simple picture: who is where, and which way they move. If it's not quite right, drag the boxes to fix it.

      3 · We slide the stencil over every clip

      Like holding a stencil over hours of video, we check every second of every camera and show you the clips where it fits best.

      4 · AI confirms the real ones

      A video AI watches the best matches and answers yes, no or not sure, with a one-line reason. Rejected clips stay visible, so you can judge for yourself.

      5 · Sweep and build a case

      Not sure what to look for? A safety sweep searches every camera for risky moments on its own. Press Add to case on any clip to keep it as evidence, then create one case report.
